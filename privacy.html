Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Straight talk, no fine print you need a magnifying glass for.

Last updated: August 17, 2026

On this page

1. Information we collect 2. How we use it 3. How we share it 4. Cookies & tracking 5. Data retention 6. Security 7. Your rights & choices 8. Children's privacy 9. Contact

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, information created by using SoleForge, and a small amount from other sources:

  • Account details — name, email, shipping address, and password
  • Transaction data — listings, bids, purchases, and payout details
  • Usage data — pages viewed, searches, and device or browser information
  • Verification data — photos or documents you submit when authenticating a pair

2. How we use it

We use your information to run the marketplace: processing listings and payments, authenticating sneakers, preventing fraud, delivering shipping updates, and improving search and recommendations. We also use your email to send order updates and, if you've opted in, product news.

3. How we share it

We share only what's needed to complete a transaction: your shipping details go to carriers, payment details go to our payment processor, and buyer/seller usernames are visible to each other on a completed order. We don't sell your personal information. We may share limited data with vendors who help us run authentication, hosting, or analytics, under contracts that restrict how they use it.

4. Cookies & tracking

SoleForge uses cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the marketplace is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; turning some off may affect how the site works.

5. Data retention

We keep account and transaction data for as long as your account is active and for a period after, to meet tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention obligations. You can request deletion of your account at any time; some records may be retained where the law requires it.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and regular reviews to protect your data. No system is completely immune to risk, so we also ask you to use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication.

7. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to opt out of marketing emails at any time. To exercise any of these, email us at privacy@soleforge.com.

8. Children's privacy

SoleForge is not directed at children, and you must be 18 or older to hold an account. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach our privacy team at privacy@soleforge.com or visit our contact page.
